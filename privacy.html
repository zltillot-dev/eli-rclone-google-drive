Privacy Policy

This is a personal application used by its owner to access Google Drive through Rclone.

Google account authorization is used solely to access and manage the owner's Google Drive files.

The application does not operate a separate cloud storage service or intentionally collect personal information for advertising or analytics.

Rclone stores authorization credentials locally on the owner's computer and communicates with Google's services to access Google Drive.

The owner can revoke access through their Google Account security settings.
