Purpose

This application is a private, personal integration used by its owner to access Google Drive through the open-source Rclone software.

This integration is intended solely for the account owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive Access

The application accesses Google Drive only after the account owner grants authorization through Google's authentication process.

Data Handling

Files are accessed and managed through Rclone. This integration does not operate a separate cloud storage service.

Availability

Access depends on Google Drive availability, internet connectivity, and the operation of the Rclone software.

Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke Google Drive authorization at any time through their Google Account security settings.
